Support

Something not working, or an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Include your speaker model and, if Neos shows something odd, what Settings › Diagnostics › Copy All gives you.

Neos doesn't find my speaker

The Mac and the speaker have to be on the same network. Check that Neos is allowed in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. A speaker in standby only answers when its network standby is on (often called Network Control, set to Always On).

Spotify isn't listed

On HEOS, Spotify only works through Spotify Connect, so it isn't available to apps like Neos. Play it from the Spotify app and pick your speaker as the device. The HEOS app works the same way.

A service is missing

Neos shows the services linked to the HEOS account on your speaker. Sign in to the service in the HEOS app first. A service hidden in Settings › Services won't appear in the sidebar or the search.

Album covers are missing on my music server

HEOS doesn't pass on album covers when you browse a music server's albums, only the covers of the tracks inside. Open the album and its cover appears.

A local server is slow to search, or never finds anything

Some music servers can't search. Uncheck Search for that server in Settings › Local Music Servers and Neos stops asking it.
