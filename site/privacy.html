Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Neos does not collect, store or share any personal data on servers of its own. It has no analytics, no tracking and no Neos account. The developer receives nothing about you or how you use the app.

What Neos connects to

  • Your HEOS speakers, over your local network, to find them, control playback and browse music.
  • Your music servers on your local network, when you browse them through your speaker.
  • The servers that host artwork for the music services you use (Tidal, Deezer, TuneIn and others). Your speaker hands Neos the address of each cover, and Neos downloads it from there, as any app showing that artwork would.

Your HEOS account

If you sign in to your HEOS account from Neos, the email and password go to your speaker, which signs in with Denon and Marantz's HEOS service. With "Remember me" on, Neos keeps them in your Mac's Keychain so it can sign in again. They are never sent anywhere else.

What stays on your Mac

Your last connected speaker, your settings, recent searches, custom station artwork and cached covers are stored on your Mac only. Deleting Neos removes them.
