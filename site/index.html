Your HEOS speakers,
from your Mac.

Neos plays Tidal, Deezer, TuneIn, SoundCloud and your own music server on Denon and Marantz HEOS speakers, without reaching for your phone.

Download for macOS
Free and open source · macOS 14 or later

All your music in one place

Jump from a Tidal album to a radio station to a FLAC on your NAS, from the same window.

One search for everything

Search every service at once and filter by artist, album or track.

See what's really playing

Bit depth, sample rate and codec of the current track, straight from the speaker.

The queue, your way

Add albums and tracks to the speaker's queue, remove them, or jump to any of them.

Lives in your menu bar

Play, skip and change the volume from the menu bar without opening the window.

A real Mac app

Native SwiftUI, no web wrapper, no Neos account and no tracking.

Neos needs a HEOS speaker or receiver on the same network as your Mac.
