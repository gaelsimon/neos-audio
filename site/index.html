Control your HEOS speakers
from your Mac

Neos lets you control your Denon or Marantz speakers from your Mac. Browse Tidal, Deezer, TuneIn, SoundCloud and other services linked to your HEOS account, or play music from your own music server. You can also manage the queue, switch speakers and control playback without reaching for your phone.

Download for macOS
Free and open source · Signed and notarized by Apple · macOS 14 or later

Browse your music

Switch between streaming services, your amp's inputs and your local music server.

Search across services

Search your music services in one go, then filter by service, artist, album or track.

Check track details

See the bit depth, sample rate and codec reported by your speaker.

Manage the queue

Add albums and tracks, remove items or skip to something further down the queue.

Manage your speakers

Choose a speaker, group speakers together and adjust their individual volumes.

Add radio stations

Save internet radio streams that aren't available on TuneIn and choose your own artwork.

Use the menu bar

Play, skip tracks and adjust the volume directly from the menu bar.

A native Mac app

Neos is built with SwiftUI. No web wrapper, no separate account and no tracking.

Neos needs a HEOS speaker or receiver on the same network as your Mac.
